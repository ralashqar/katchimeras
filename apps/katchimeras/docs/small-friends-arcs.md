# Petalimp and Fernip: smaller friends, sharper arcs

Status: wired into the runtime, Sept 11, 2026 (the "Proposed" columns are what ships). Ids, orders, boards and the
style mapping (gentle / curious / together, unhurried / playful / sheltered) are untouched, so
this is a strings-only pass once approved.

## The brief

Petalimp and Fernip are not main companions. Their arcs are four short visits each, not a
relationship. So:

- **One image, then the ask.** Prompts under 35 words. Every other beat one or two lines.
- **The wisps are characters.** Petty, gloating squatters who *do* things between chapters:
  they come back at night, they steal the colours, they sit on the guest book so nobody can
  read it, they sit on a mushroom for years out of spite. The friend calls them "them".
- **Choices are things you do**, never how you feel, and every reply is a joke or a jab.
- **Every chapter has a reveal and a cost.** The Mist gives something back at the end; the
  next chapter opens on what it took in revenge.
- **The friends have a comic flaw and a brave streak.** Petalimp: vain, fussy, tidies under
  fire. Fernip: deadpan, allegedly asleep, secretly the one who never gave up.
- **The payoff stays but goes light**: a one-line badge for how the player played.

The through-lines:

- **Petalimp: ground won back.** The wisps have been feeding on the garden for years. Each visit
  takes ground: the first patch, more of the beds, the way through, the last patch. Nothing in
  the lines names what the island looks like, so the art can be anything.
- **Fernip: weight lifted.** The wisps have been sitting on the grove for years. Each visit lifts
  them off more of it: the first patch, the oldest part, the way through, the far end. Fernip
  stays unhurried throughout. (The last-light twist from the first pass was cut with the visuals.)

## Petalimp

Revised twice after device reads (Sept 11, 2026). The first pass tried too hard; the second named
visuals the island art may not show (a pink bed by the gate, a bench, a path). This one is about
ground: the wisps feed on the garden, lose the first patch, pull back and dig in, are cut through,
and are cornered on the last patch. The choices are about how to go at them, not what things look
like. Chapter titles are unchanged.
