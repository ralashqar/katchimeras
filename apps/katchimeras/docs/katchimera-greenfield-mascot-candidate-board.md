# Katchimera Greenfield Mascot Candidate Board

This document is the first output of the mascot-first process.

It is intentionally not constrained by the current Katchimera catalog.
The point is to find the strongest mascot characters first, then decide whether they:

- replace an existing mascot
- rebuild an existing niche
- become entirely new flagship characters

Related references:

- `docs/katchimera-mascot-creation-process.md`
- `docs/katchimera-shareability-framework.md`
- `docs/katchimera-flagship-mascot-roster.md`

## 1. Goal

Generate a greenfield slate of mascot concepts optimized for:

- TikTok / Reels / Shorts
- brand memorability
- social posting
- clear lifestyle identity
- app fit

## 2. Candidate Pool

## 3. Best 8 Shortlist

These are the strongest candidates after applying:

- one-line premise strength
- visual gimmick clarity
- lifestyle recognizability
- comedy repeatability
- app fit

### 1. Baristabbit

- reason: strongest universal cafe mascot with obvious bit and iconic silhouette
- likely action: rebuild / replace current cafe lead

### 2. Crumbelle

- reason: pastry-diva energy is more character-rich than generic bakery cute
- likely action: rebuild bakery flagship around this attitude

### 3. Mossmischief

- reason: park goblin is more content-rich than a purely gentle nature spirit
- likely action: evolve current Mossprout direction rather than replace the niche

### 4. Signalhop

- reason: best urban-chaos anchor, very strong short-form reaction potential
- likely action: likely replaces Neonpoko/Signalbun as the cleaner flagship form

### 5. Bedrotte

- reason: massive meme potential and highly relatable lifestyle niche
- likely action: new flagship, no current equivalent strong enough

### 6. Errandimp

- reason: errands are underrepresented but extremely relatable
- likely action: new flagship and strong adult-life content bridge

### 7. Shelfself

- reason: bookstore overthinker is very internet-native and identity-rich
- likely action: new culture flagship

### 8. Doggoblin

- reason: broad audience, highly visual, easy comedy, strong social behavior cues
- likely action: new social-outdoor flagship

## 4. Best 4 To Prototype First

If the goal is maximum upside quickly, prototype these first:

1. `Baristabbit`
2. `Bedrotte`
3. `Signalhop`
4. `Errandimp`

Why this four:

- very broad lifestyle coverage
- very clear comedy engines
- very strong visual gimmicks
- minimal overlap
- strong contrast between cozy, chaos, and adult-life humor

## 6. Recommended Production Sprint

### Step 1

Take the top 8 shortlist and create one-line mascot concept sheets for each:

- final working name
- archetype
- lifestyle niche
- encounter mapping
- visual gimmick
- comedy engine

### Step 2

Cut to the best 4 after checking:

- thumbnail read
- silent reaction potential
- motion loop potential
- social-media explanation ease

### Step 3

For those 4, create:

- visual prompt sheet
- emote sheet
- 10 TikTok scenario prompts each
- reveal-copy style examples

### Step 4

Only after that, map them back into:

- encounter seeds
- variant structure
- onboarding / app use

## 7. Final Recommendation

Do not lock the flagship roster from the current catalog yet.

Use this candidate board to run a proper mascot-first selection pass.

If forced to choose one sentence:

- `Baristabbit, Bedrotte, Signalhop, and Errandimp are the strongest greenfield starting point for a viral Katchimera mascot universe.`
