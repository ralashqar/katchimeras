# Wisp art bible

Wisps are tiny collectible fragments of lived experience. They share the Katchimeras cozy premium toy finish, but they are not miniature Katchimeras and never become virtual pets.

## Identity contract

- One freestanding creature on a canonical square canvas.
- Readable at 32–48 px and at 20–30% of the Egg Avatar's height.
- One dominant motif, one simple face, one contained glow.
- Zero to two tiny limbs; fins, leaflets, rays, vapor, petals, or wings are preferred.
- Broader, simpler anatomy and materially less detail than a Katchimera.
- No environment, floor, platform, cast shadow, UI, text, watermark, costume clutter, or second creature.

## Style contract: `katchimeras-wisp-cozy-toy-v1`

Reference authority is ordered:

1. An approved Wisp anchor controls scale, facial economy, silhouette simplicity, and canvas registration.
2. Baristabbit controls broad rounded forms, softly painted materials, restrained highlights, and friendly premium rendering.
3. The exact Today runtime background controls warm light, gentle saturation, and low contrast.
4. The Classic Egg controls relative scale only and must never be generated into the Wisp asset.

Use matte resin, ceramic, paper, cloud, flame, leaf, stone, or pastry materials with simplified shading. Eyes must be friendly and graphic, never wet, hollow, glassy, or photoreal. The attached concept sheet may guide taxonomy and charm, but it is not production pixel authority.

## Approval gates

1. The silhouette and face read immediately at 40 px.
2. The creature remains clearly smaller and simpler than a Katchimera.
3. There is exactly one dominant real-life motif.
4. No background, contact shadow, pedestal, stray prop, text, or extra anatomy survives.
5. The subject is centered with generous transparent padding and clean alpha corners.
6. A compatibility sheet beside Classic Egg, a Katchimera, the You grid, hatch stage, and daily card passes visual review.
7. Palette, model, prompt, references, hashes, and matte provenance are recorded in the manifest.

Reject and regenerate rather than manually repainting, erasing, cropping, recentring, or reconstructing generated pixels.
