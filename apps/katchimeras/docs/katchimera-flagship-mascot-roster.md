# Katchimera Flagship Mascot Roster

This document defines the small set of Katchimeras that should be treated as:

- flagship brand mascots
- recurring short-form video characters
- onboarding anchors
- app-marketing leads

These are not just strong creatures inside the catalog.
They are character assets.

## 1. Goal

The flagship mascot tier should do three jobs at once:

1. represent recognizable life patterns inside the app
2. feel cute and postable as static art
3. perform well as funny short-form characters in TikTok, Reels, and Shorts

That means these mascots should be:

- silhouette-clear
- expressive
- easy to animate
- easy to describe
- easy to remember
- emotionally lovable

## 2. Roster Selection Rules

A flagship mascot should have:

- one instantly readable lifestyle niche
- one instantly readable visual gimmick
- one simple repeatable personality bit
- one strong motion language
- one clear comedy engine

If a creature is beautiful but does not have a repeatable character bit, it is not a flagship mascot.

## 3. Phase 1 Roster

Important:

This roster is a `provisional candidate slate`, not a permanent commitment to the current catalog.

These are the existing Katchimeras that currently look closest to flagship material.

The final flagship cast should be selected using:

- `docs/katchimera-mascot-creation-process.md`

That means:

- some of these should survive
- some should be rebuilt
- some may be replaced by newly invented mascot-first characters

Recommended current candidate slate:

1. `Lattelet`
2. `Crumbun`
3. `Mossprout`
4. `Neonpoko`
5. `Sprintail`
6. `Hayhorn`
7. `Ironette`
8. `Hushling`

Why this group:

- broad lifestyle coverage
- strong visual variety
- clear niche identities
- both cozy and high-energy personalities
- good mix of everyday and aspirational

## 4. Character Specs

### Lattelet

Role:

- the coffee-ritual mascot

Lifestyle niche:

- cafe regular
- soft-start person
- comfort-and-routine user

Visual hook:

- ceramic cup hood
- latte swirl top
- caramel core

Personality:

- over-serious about coffee
- tiny barista energy
- warm but slightly fussy

Comedy engine:

- acts like coffee rituals are sacred
- judges bad coffee silently
- becomes emotionally stable only after first sip

Motion language:

- tiny careful steps
- cup wobble
- steam puff reactions
- satisfied blink after sipping

Best video formats:

- morning routine parody
- “before coffee / after coffee”
- cafe ranking humor
- ritual-core content

Virality reason:

- instantly understandable
- highly memeable
- extremely repeatable

## 5. Character System

Each flagship mascot should have:

- one `core pose`
- one `idle loop`
- one `surprised reaction`
- one `annoyed reaction`
- one `delighted reaction`

This creates reusable animation grammar across social content.

## 6. Content Pillars

Use the roster across these content buckets.

### Everyday life bits

- coffee rituals
- late starts
- weather moods
- commuting
- gym avoidance or enthusiasm
- bakery temptation

### Group dynamics

- Lattelet vs Crumbun over snacks
- Sprintail trying to motivate Hushling
- Neonpoko overstimulating Mossprout
- Hayhorn acting as the practical one
- Ironette making everything too dramatic

### Lifestyle identity

- “which Katchimera are you today?”
- “you are a Lattelet if…”
- “Neonpoko night vs Mossprout morning”

### Trend adaptation

- reaction audios
- before / after routines
- “POV” formats
- tier lists
- duets
- stitchable personality clips

## 7. Recurring Humor Structure

The safest recurring structure is:

- cute design
- recognizable lifestyle behavior
- slightly exaggerated emotional reaction

Examples:

- Lattelet hearing the espresso machine
- Hushling seeing a 9 PM plan
- Neonpoko at a crosswalk
- Crumbun spotting a pastry box
- Sprintail after someone says “I might go for a run”

## 8. What Makes A Mascot TikTok-Ready

The strongest short-form mascot has:

- a one-line personality summary
- a visual feature that reads instantly
- a motion pattern that loops well
- a reaction face that is funny without dialogue
- a lifestyle situation people already recognize

If the mascot needs explanation, it is not ready.

## 9. Implementation Guidance

For flagship mascots, design and generation should prioritize:

- facial acting over fine detail
- silhouette over texture complexity
- looping motion over static elegance
- clear character bit over broad abstraction

This is stricter than the wider Katchimera catalog.

## 10. Priority Recommendation

If only four mascots are developed first for video:

1. `Lattelet`
2. `Crumbun`
3. `Mossprout`
4. `Neonpoko`

Why:

- strongest instant read
- broadest shareability
- highest comedy range
- easiest to make cute and funny quickly

If six are developed:

Add:

5. `Sprintail`
6. `Hushling`

## 11. Final Direction

The flagship mascot tier should not behave like a random creature catalog.

It should behave like a cast.

The goal is:

- `recognizable characters with repeatable bits`

not:

- `a pile of good-looking creatures`

When in doubt, choose:

- stronger personality
- clearer silhouette
- simpler joke
- more recognizable lifestyle behavior
