## App Store description

**Every day you live becomes a little creature. Tonight, meet today's.**

Katchimeras turns your real day — the coffee stop, the park walk, the night in — into a small magical character, hatched each evening from an egg that quietly forms while you live.

No journaling homework. No streaks. No guilt. You don't feed Katchimeras your day — it notices on its own.

**HOW IT WORKS**
Through the day, an egg gathers shape from the places you go, the steps you take, and the photos you snap. Add a moment by hand if you feel like it — or don't. At your chosen evening hour, the egg is ready. Tap, and the day hatches.

**WHO YOU'LL MEET**
A coffee-shop morning brings Baristabbit, the over-serious little barista. A green detour wakes Mossprout. A night at the movies, Flickerbun. And on the days you cancel everything and stay under the blanket? Bedrotte appears — because rest counts here, too.

Here's the magic: come back to a ritual, and the same character comes back to you. Your third café morning this week isn't a new stranger — it's Baristabbit, visit three, and it remembers.

**A REFLECTION, NOT A REPORT**
Each hatch arrives with a few words in your character's voice about what your day actually was — "a third time finding the same corner, the same warmth." Not stats. Not scores. Something that sounds like being seen.

**KEEP IT. SHARE IT.**
Every hatched day becomes a postcard — your creature, its words, the shape your day drew on the map — made to be kept, or sent to someone who'll say "this app knows you."

**YOUR DAYS, GATHERING**
Flip back through the week. Watch your collection grow into something no one else could have: a cast of characters that is, somehow, exactly you. The egg even lives on your home screen, so today is always quietly becoming someone.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
Your days, places, and moments stay on your phone. No account. No feed. No one watching but your creatures.
