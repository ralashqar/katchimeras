## 1. Priority Set

These are the first four mascots to prototype:

1. `Baristabbit`
2. `Bedrotte`
3. `Signalhop`
4. `Errandimp`

Why these four:

- broadest daily-life relevance
- strongest comedy engines
- best visual variety
- strongest social-video potential
- clean app mapping

## 2. Production Format

Each sheet includes:

- role
- final name recommendation
- design direction
- motion rules
- app mapping
- reveal-copy direction
- 10 short-form prompts

## 3. Baristabbit

### Core role

- flagship coffee mascot

### Final name recommendation

- `Baristabbit`

Why:

- instantly readable
- says both `barista` and `rabbit`
- stronger mascot identity than a softer name like `Lattelet`

Possible alternates:

- `Cremabbit`
- `Mugbun`
- `Foambit`

### One-line premise

- tiny barista gremlin who treats coffee like religion

### Lifestyle niche

- cafe regular
- soft-start user
- morning ritual person

### App mapping

- primary encounter: `coffee_shop`
- secondary encounter: `coffee_home`
- mood: `cozy focus`
- pattern meaning: `ritual` / `return`

### Visual direction

- oversized ceramic cup hood
- latte-foam ears or swirl crown
- warm caramel chest core
- short grounded bunny body
- tiny apron-scarf or steam ribbon accent

### Must keep

- cup silhouette read
- coffee ritual read
- barista seriousness
- bunny agility

### Avoid

- looking too food-object only
- becoming generic latte mascot
- too many props
- realistic rabbit anatomy

### Expression range

- neutral: focused tiny professional
- delighted: eyes widen at perfect crema
- annoyed: silent judgment
- shocked: cup wobble panic
- sleepy: uncharged before coffee

### Motion language

- tiny precise steps
- cup wobble when startled
- steam puff sighs
- ear-swirl bounce
- ceremonial sip pause

### Core animation beats

- idle: checks invisible coffee setup, blinks once, tiny steam release
- delighted: little hop, swirl ears lift, core glows warmer
- annoyed: half-lidded stare, tiny foot tap
- panic: cup tilt correction and rapid paw reset
- satisfied: long blink after sip

### Reveal copy direction

Good lines:

- `Warmth, ritual, and a little return shaped this one.`
- `Your coffee habits are starting to leave a signature.`
- `A familiar stop gave the day its glow.`

### 10 TikTok prompt ideas

1. `Baristabbit before first coffee vs after first coffee`
2. `Baristabbit judging latte art like a tiny Michelin critic`
3. `When the cafe gets your usual order wrong`
4. `POV: your morning ritual has unionized`
5. `Baristabbit hearing the espresso machine from another room`
6. `Ranking coffees Baristabbit would and would not respect`
7. `Baristabbit at home trying to recreate cafe energy`
8. `The emotional journey of waiting for cold brew`
9. `Baristabbit reacting to someone saying “instant is fine”`
10. `Which coffee type are you according to Baristabbit`

## 4. Bedrotte

### Core role

- flagship recovery / homebody mascot

### Final name recommendation

- `Bedrotte`

Why:

- instantly meme-readable
- high internet-native humor
- already carries the anti-plan bit

Possible alternates:

- `Blankette`
- `Snuzloom`
- `Pillowlit`

### One-line premise

- anti-plan blanket royalty who thinks recovery is sacred

### Lifestyle niche

- homebody
- recovery-day user
- soft-evening / low-social-energy user

### App mapping

- primary encounter: `home`
- secondary encounter: `calm evening` / `rest day`
- mood: `restorative`
- pattern meaning: `recovery` / `self-protection`

### Visual direction

- blanket mantle or cocoon wrap
- pillow-crown silhouette
- sleepy ember belly core
- droopy but regal posture
- soft rounded paws barely peeking out

### Must keep

- anti-plan energy
- immediate bedtime read
- cute royalty contrast
- plush silhouette

### Avoid

- ghost-horror read
- generic sleepy blob
- too elegant and not funny enough
- too slouchy to animate cleanly

### Expression range

- neutral: sleepy entitlement
- delighted: nest-building joy
- annoyed: offended by plans
- shocked: 9 PM notification stare
- protective: wraps into cocoon around comfort item

### Motion language

- hover-drift or soft shuffle
- blanket wrap motion
- slow eye squint
- pillow flop
- dramatic refusal turn

### Core animation beats

- idle: settles deeper into blanket, slow blink, warm belly pulse
- delighted: wiggle into softer position
- annoyed: blanket flare and deadpan stare
- shocked: frozen wide-eye stare, then immediate retreat
- relieved: full burrito wrap and content sigh

### Reveal copy direction

Good lines:

- `Recovery became the part of the day that stayed visible.`
- `Stillness shaped this one more than effort did.`
- `Today asked less of you, and that mattered too.`

### 10 TikTok prompt ideas

1. `Bedrotte reacting to a 9 PM “you coming?” text`
2. `How Bedrotte prepares for absolutely no plans`
3. `POV: the blanket has chosen you`
4. `Bedrotte ranking excuses to stay in`
5. `When someone says “it’s just a quick drink”`
6. `Different levels of social battery according to Bedrotte`
7. `Bedrotte discovering that tomorrow is free`
8. `What recovery days actually look like`
9. `Bedrotte protecting one soft corner of the bed like a throne`
10. `You are a Bedrotte if…`

## 5. Signalhop

### Core role

- flagship urban-chaos mascot

### Final name recommendation

- `Signalhop`

Why:

- clearer than `Neonpoko`
- more behavior-driven than a pure style name
- strong movement and city-signal read

Possible alternates:

- `Crossbun`
- `Blinkhop`
- `Tokihop`

### One-line premise

- overstimulated city bunny who treats every crossing like a cinematic event

### Lifestyle niche

- night owl
- city wanderer
- after-dark social user

### App mapping

- primary encounter: `shibuya_crossing` or dense urban route
- secondary encounter: `night city walk`
- mood: `electric calm`
- pattern meaning: `identity` / `intensity`

### Visual direction

- signal-light ears
- neon tail loop
- compact rounded bounce body
- crosswalk-stripe accents
- midnight glow core

### Must keep

- city overstimulation
- fast reactive silhouette
- bunny readability
- glowing signal motif

### Avoid

- generic cyber mascot
- too much streetwear clutter
- sticker-neon flatness
- full combat or gamer-hero read

### Expression range

- neutral: alert city scan
- delighted: chaos is happening
- annoyed: signal wait frustration
- shocked: huge wide-eye crosswalk freeze
- smug: caught the perfect city moment

### Motion language

- bounce-stop-bounce rhythm
- quick head snaps
- neon tail flicks
- freeze-turn reactions
- signal blink loops

### Core animation beats

- idle: scans left-right, tail pulses, one ear blinks like a signal
- delighted: double-hop and neon flare
- annoyed: tiny stomp at red light
- shocked: full freeze with giant eye flare
- confident: strut-hop through a lit crossing

### Reveal copy direction

Good lines:

- `Night motion gave the day its sharpest glow.`
- `The city left a stronger mark on you tonight.`
- `A louder route shaped this one into focus.`

### 10 TikTok prompt ideas

1. `Signalhop at a crosswalk when the countdown starts`
2. `POV: the city is a little too awake and you are kind of into it`
3. `Signalhop reacting to different kinds of street noise`
4. `Main-character crossing energy according to Signalhop`
5. `Signalhop in quiet suburbia vs Signalhop in downtown neon`
6. `When the train doors close right in front of you`
7. `Signalhop navigating crowds like it is an extreme sport`
8. `How city people recharge according to Signalhop`
9. `Signalhop discovering a new shortcut at night`
10. `You are a Signalhop if…`

## 6. Errandimp

### Core role

- flagship adult-life / practical-day mascot

### Final name recommendation

- `Errandimp`

Why:

- immediately funny
- broadly relatable
- fills a strong everyday-life gap in the current lineup

Possible alternates:

- `Cartbit`
- `Listling`
- `Aisleimp`

### One-line premise

- practical-day gremlin who treats tiny productivity like heroic conquest

### Lifestyle niche

- errands
- groceries
- adult-life chores
- productive reset days

### App mapping

- primary encounter: `grocery`
- secondary encounter: `mall` / errands loop
- mood: `grounded`
- pattern meaning: `follow-through` / `care`

### Visual direction

- checklist tail
- cart-wheel feet
- tote-bag or basket shell accents
- produce-color pops
- receipt-strip ribbon

### Must keep

- practical-day recognition
- tiny chaos of normal adult life
- lovable competence
- highly readable props

### Avoid

- becoming generic shopping mascot
- looking too brand-commercial
- overloading with grocery items
- losing the imp energy

### Expression range

- neutral: determined errand mode
- delighted: crosses item off invisible list
- annoyed: one extra task appeared
- shocked: forgot the main item
- triumphant: made it home with everything

### Motion language

- quick determined shuffle
- cart-wheel bounce
- list-check gestures
- tote-swing counterbalance
- tiny victory pose

### Core animation beats

- idle: checks invisible list, nods, shuffles forward
- delighted: little fist clench and cart-wheel kick
- annoyed: receipt tail flick
- shocked: freeze, look back, panic pivot
- triumphant: tote lift and core flash

### Reveal copy direction

Good lines:

- `Care took the shape of follow-through today.`
- `Practical choices held the day together.`
- `Even the ordinary work of living left a clear mark.`

### 10 TikTok prompt ideas

1. `Errandimp when you finally do the one task you delayed for 3 days`
2. `The emotional phases of grocery shopping according to Errandimp`
3. `POV: you left home for one thing and came back with six`
4. `Errandimp after remembering the reusable bags`
5. `Adult life wins that feel bigger than they should`
6. `Errandimp when one extra stop gets added to the route`
7. `The final item on the list as a boss battle`
8. `Errandimp ranking chores by emotional damage`
9. `You are an Errandimp if…`
10. `Errandimp celebrating a fully completed list like it won the championship`

## 7. Recommended Sprint Output

For each of the four above, the next concrete asset pack should include:

1. one hero illustration prompt
2. one simplified mascot turnaround prompt
3. one facial expression sheet
4. one idle-loop brief
5. one reaction-pack brief
6. ten short-form content prompts

## 8. Final Recommendation

Lock these four as the first prototype wave:

- `Baristabbit`
- `Bedrotte`
- `Signalhop`
- `Errandimp`

They are the strongest combination of:

- brand character potential
- social-video utility
- clear lifestyle reflection
- easy explanation inside and outside the app
