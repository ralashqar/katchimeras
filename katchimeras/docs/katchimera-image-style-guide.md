# Katchimera Image Style Guide

## Recommended Default Style

Use:

- `premium stylized 3D mascot creature render`
- `premium stylized game mascot CGI`

Do not use:

- fully realistic animal rendering
- bright toy-commercial Clash Royale styling
- exaggerated Fortnite hero proportions
- heavy super-deformed chibi proportions
- photorealistic textures or cinematic realism

## Why This Style Fits

KatchaDeck is calm, reflective, and slightly magical. The creatures should feel emotionally adoptable, collectible, and premium, but not loud, childish, or overly gamey.

Best fit:

- premium stylized mascot feel
- cartoony shape design with believable premium shading
- simplified game-character materials with realistic lighting response
- head-dominant, chunkier proportions
- much larger eyes and more graphic faces
- warmer emotional readability
- dynamic pose and appealing expression
- strong silhouettes at card size

This should feel closer to:

- premium mobile-game mascot rendering
- Fortnite-level silhouette clarity without combat energy
- Royal Match-level facial charm and readability
- bold stylized 3D character polish

It should feel less like:

- mobile battle game splash art
- cheap toy-box comedy fantasy
- hyper-real monster concept art
- moody premium figurine realism

## Visual Rules

- one creature only
- centered or slightly three-quarter pose
- readable silhouette
- premium moody background
- emotionally readable face
- no text
- no UI frame baked into image
- no cluttered scenery
- no multiple characters

## Material Direction

- soft plush, stylized resin, clean ceramic, moss, bark, shell, pastry texture
- simplified material rendering rather than realistic surface complexity
- clean key light plus soft fill and soft rim light
- grounded contact shadows and richer eye gloss
- premium polish without photoreal textures or cheap plastic toy shine

## Shape Language

- bold readable silhouette first
- head-dominant rounded mascot body language
- dramatically oversized eye readability
- one or two signature details only
- one signature detail should be deliberately overscaled
- stronger mouth, brow, and cheek expression than before
- pose should always feel asymmetrical and animated, never neutral
- avoid over-accessorizing
- avoid armor overload
- creature must still read clearly as one family across habitats and stages
- do not go fully chibi, but do push toward more exaggerated cartoony proportions

## Pose Families

- `bounce pose`: lifted limb, springy lean, cheerful momentum
- `mid-hop pose`: airborne or almost-airborne read with off-axis twist
- `proud pose`: chest up, slight torso turn, confident tilt
- `clingy pose`: tucked limbs, rounded inward silhouette, attached charm
- `sneak pose`: low asymmetrical posture with alert eyes
- `slouchy cozy pose`: relaxed lean, sleepy head tilt, soft massing

## Expression Presets

- `delighted`: lifted cheeks, bright eyes, open warmth
- `smug-cute`: confident mouth asymmetry, playful brow read
- `shy`: tucked mouth, softer lids, inward charm
- `sleepy`: half-lidded eyes, relaxed cheeks, low-energy appeal
- `startled`: widened eyes, lifted brows, readable surprise
- `proud`: brighter eyes, firmer brow, self-satisfied charm
- `overfocused`: tiny concentrated mouth, determined brows, competent little gremlin energy

## Render Recipe

- stylize anatomy and pose aggressively
- keep body masses broad and simple
- use real studio-light logic: key, fill, rim, bounce, contact shadow
- spend the richest shading on eyes, glow core, and hero motif
- keep the rest of the body cleaner and more graphic

## Stage Direction

- `sprout`: simpler, smaller, cleaner, more approachable, slightly cuter
- `shaped`: richer secondary forms, stronger markings
- `ascended`: fuller aura, stronger habitat integration, more majestic silhouette without becoming harsh

## Habitat Direction

- `home`: warmer, softer, sanctuary-lit
- `cozy`: amber, plush, intimate
- `urban`: sleek, signal-lit, reflective, but still companion-like
- `nature`: mossy, wind-touched, rooted
- `waterside`: airy, rippled, horizon-lit

## Prompt Pattern

Recommended pattern:

1. family identity
2. stage identity
3. habitat modifier
4. signature visual traits
5. material and lighting direction
6. camera / composition
7. pose / expression
8. quality / rendering style
9. negatives

## Negatives

- no text
- no watermark
- no frame
- no extra creatures
- no human
- no weapon
- no UI overlay
- no heavy chibi proportions
- no aggressive monster face
- no plastic toy finish
- no photorealism
- no realistic anatomy
- no hyper-detailed texture realism
- no moody cinematic realism

## Final Recommendation

Default prompt style:

- `premium stylized 3D mascot creature render`
- `premium stylized game mascot CGI`
- `Fortnite-inspired silhouette clarity without combat energy`
- `Royal Match-inspired facial readability and charm`
- `clean stylized lighting`
- `dramatically oversized glossy luminous eyes`

- `exaggerated chunky readable proportions`
