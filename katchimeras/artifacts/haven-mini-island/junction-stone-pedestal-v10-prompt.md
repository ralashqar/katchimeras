# Haven junction stone pedestal v10

Generated with the built-in image-generation workflow. The v9 pedestal is the
subject reference and the Square Haven Mossprout master is the project style
reference.

The production prompt locks the asset to a true orthographic elevated view:

- opposing horizontal construction edges remain parallel;
- left and right construction edges remain parallel;
- the rear and front of the upper platform have identical width;
- the amber perimeter rim keeps a constant visual width;
- there is no vanishing point, perspective taper, wide-angle distortion, or
  trapezoidal footprint;
- the deep front fascia and centered two-tier pedestal retain strong sculptural
  depth without relying on perspective convergence.

The asset remains an empty amber-and-cream toy-stone platform on a flat magenta
chroma background. Characters, trays, order items, stairs, bridges, vegetation,
text, shadows, and background scenery are excluded.

The magenta background is removed locally with the imagegen chroma-key helper;
the alpha master is then packaged into the runtime 512px and 256px WebP tiers.
