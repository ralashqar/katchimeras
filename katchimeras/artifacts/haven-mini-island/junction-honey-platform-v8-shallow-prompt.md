# Haven junction honey platform v8 — shallow plinth

Generated with the built-in image-editing workflow from the approved v7
parallel platform and the user's compact platform reference.

The pale inset top, honey block rim, parallel orthographic construction,
materials, lighting, and empty-surface rules remain unchanged. The separate
terracotta floating-rock/cliff tier is removed completely and replaced by the
rim's short continuous honey/caramel structural fascia with a flat lower edge.

Runtime presentation also reduces each base from 160 to 140 world pixels while
retaining the existing island centers, tray frames, order contents, and serving
targets.

No creature, tray, item, stairs, connection, bridge, cliff, vegetation, text,
or UI is baked into the asset. It was generated over flat `#ff00ff` and
extracted locally to alpha.
