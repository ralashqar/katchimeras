# Haven junction stone pedestal v9

Generated from scratch with the built-in image-generation workflow. The user's
attached platform is the strict structural, projection, depth, and silhouette
reference; the Square Haven Mossprout master supplies the softer molded-toy
materials, simplified texture, palette restraint, and lighting.

The production asset contains:

- a near-square amber block rim with clipped rounded corners;
- a recessed cream floor made from a few large irregular paving stones;
- a thick, readable front fascia;
- a centered narrower two-step caramel structural pedestal beneath it;
- parallel orthographic/axonometric top geometry with no vanishing point.

The pedestal depth is architectural rather than a natural rocky cliff. Runtime
size remains 140 world pixels, and the separate trays retain their existing
frames and serving targets.

No creature, tray, item, stairs, connection, bridge, vegetation, text, or UI is
baked into the asset. It was generated over flat `#ff00ff` and extracted
locally to alpha.
