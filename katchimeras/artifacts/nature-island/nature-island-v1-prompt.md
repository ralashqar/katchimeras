## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: isolated production-ready decorative floating-island game asset for the Katchimeras Haven world map.

Generate a brand-new small ornamental nature islet inspired by the supplied waterfall-garden reference, rendered precisely in the established visual language and camera of the Mossprout base island reference.

Create one compact self-contained floating nature islet, significantly smaller and simpler than the Mossprout base island. Include a single charming broad-canopy tree, a tiny turquoise spring or pond fed by one short waterfall over two or three chunky cream rocks, a few lily pads, mossy grass, and restrained white daisies with one small pink accent cluster. Use a chunky rounded hanging earth-and-stone underside matching the Mossprout island construction.

Use a cozy polished 3D toy-diorama style with soft broad bevels, low-poly sculpted forms, very low texture detail, clean simple materials, large readable shapes, and gentle ambient occlusion. Preserve the Mossprout lime green, warm honey earth, cream stone, and clear turquoise-water palette.

Center the islet on a square canvas with generous even padding and the full silhouette visible. Match the Mossprout reference's elevated front-isometric near-orthographic camera and camera height. Use an orthographic game-asset projection with parallel corresponding edges, no perspective convergence, no wide-angle distortion, and no dramatic foreshortening.

Use the same soft warm upper-left studio lighting and cozy daylight mood as the Mossprout reference. Keep highlights restrained and contact shading contained within the asset.

Render on a perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` chroma-key background. The background must have no gradient, texture, lighting variation, floor plane, cast shadow, contact shadow, reflection, haze, or vignette.

Do not include a creature, character, house, lantern, fence, bridge, stairs, path, tray, table, chair, UI, icon, text, watermark, clouds, sky, distant islands, or extra props. Nature only. Use fewer flowers and less foliage clutter than the composition reference.
