# Baristabbit café island generation prompt

## Reference roles

- Image 1: `mossprout-main-environment-1024.png`, strict structural base for
  footprint, front-isometric camera, scale, padding, cliff depth, and silhouette.
- Image 2: live runtime `floating_neighborhood_v2_baristabbit_hex_tile.webp`,
  theme reference for café identity, palette, props, and material language.

## Prompt

Create one standalone Baristabbit café floating island for the Haven game map.
Replace Mossprout-specific contents from Image 1 with café contents from Image
2 while preserving Image 1's rounded-square footprint, exact elevated
front-isometric camera, apparent scale, padding, deep layered floating cliff,
soft silhouette, and side-connection height.

Place a small cream-and-terracotta café cottage at the rear center with a broad
striped canopy and a generic sculpted cup emblem, with no words. Add one chunky
espresso counter, one small round table with two stools, and two amber lanterns.
Keep the central and front-middle patio spacious, with a simple circular paving
motif for a separately rendered live character. Wall the island perimeter in
the same structural manner as Mossprout, but leave one unmistakable centered
opening at the front. From that opening, add the same kind of short chunky
stairway descending over the deep front cliff. Keep the right/east edge near
mid-height clear as a bridge landing, but do not draw a bridge. Use only sparse
large shrubs and a few simple edge flowers.

Strict art style: cozy 3D toy diorama, matte clay, broad soft bevels, chunky
simplified geometry, very low detail, extremely low texture detail, and soft
warm studio light. Use a restrained cream, terracotta, caramel, moss, sage, and
amber palette. No realism, grain, scratches, tiny foliage, busy decoration,
character, animal, egg, board grid, UI, text, watermark, bridge, extra island,
sky, or ground.

Render on one perfectly uniform flat `#ff00ff` chroma-key background edge to
edge. No background shadow, gradient, texture, floor, reflection, glow, or
lighting variation. Never use magenta inside the island. Keep a crisp complete
silhouette, no clipping, and generous even padding.
