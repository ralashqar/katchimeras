# Shellio trophy generation prompts

Both sheets were generated with the built-in image generator using these references:

- `assets/images/katchimeras/cutouts/shellio.png` — primary character materials and palette
- `assets/images/katchimeras/world/hex/floating_neighborhood_v2_shellio_hex_tile_512.webp` — beach-home environment and prop language
- `assets/images/katchimeras/world/objects/places/tidepool_basin.webp` — existing tidepool keepsake scale and finish

## Shared direction

Create isolated Shellio keepsakes matching the supplied premium cozy miniature 3D Katchimeras art: rounded handcrafted clay, shell, driftwood, woven canvas and smooth beach stone; soft diffuse light; seafoam turquoise, aqua, warm sand, coral, pearl cream and driftwood brown. They are cherished waterside objects, never generic cups or medals. Use a perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` chroma-key background. No grid lines, cast/contact shadows, floor plane, reflections, characters, text, numbers, generic awards, photorealism, glass, translucent water, watermark, or magenta inside objects.

Every subject must occupy no more than 68% of its cell, remain centered, and keep every object and decoration wholly inside a completely empty uninterrupted chroma gutter.
