## Shared production prompt

Create isolated premium soft rounded 3D Katchimeras keepsakes. Burnt orange, honey gold, deep teal leaves, cream, warm oak and copper; carved wood, matte ceramic and woven canvas. Use a perfectly flat uniform `#ff00ff` background. Exact grid; one compact connected object per cell; subject no more than 68% of its cell; at least 10% uninterrupted empty gutter on every side. Nothing touches a boundary. No cast/contact shadows, floor, reflections, text, numbers, characters, panels, scenery, generic medals or trophy cups.
