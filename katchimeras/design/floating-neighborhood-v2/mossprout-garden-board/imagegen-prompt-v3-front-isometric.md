# Mossprout tall garden board - v3 front-isometric art direction

Use `geometry-guide-v3-front-isometric-1024x1536.png` as the geometry
authority. Its diagram colours are annotations rather than physical materials.
Use the user's portrait garden-board reference for composition and front cliff
depth, the populated merge-board reference for cell material, and the selected
v1 garden source for Katchimeras materials.

Render one continuous garden island spanning two vertically stacked hex
positions in a symmetric, straight-on front-isometric camera. The rear garden
and wall remain visible at the top, while the deep front cliff faces the viewer
at the bottom. Preserve the four diagonal end-edge connection segments and
keep the two long middle sides sealed.

The central board must contain exactly six columns and seven rows: 42 empty,
fully visible rounded grass cells. Keep the board centered, unobstructed, and
slightly narrower toward the back. Set dressing stays outside it: a small
waterfall pond, a tiny blue-roof hut, two lanterns, broad-leaf bushes, rounded
trees, and restrained flower clusters.

Style: polished cozy 3D mobile-game toy diorama; matte clay; broad soft bevels;
chunky honey sandstone; simple readable silhouettes; warm upper-left daylight;
low texture noise. No items, characters, creatures, eggs, labels, UI,
watermarks, neighbouring islands, bridges, or middle-side connections.

Generate on a perfectly uniform `#ff00ff` chroma-key background with no floor,
external shadow, gradient, clouds, or atmospheric variation. Keep the island
centered with generous padding and do not use magenta in the subject.

The selected generation required two constrained correction edits: first to
retain seven rows and remove guide colours, then to split the board into six
equal columns. The final selected source is
`mossprout-garden-board-v3-front-isometric-source.png`.
