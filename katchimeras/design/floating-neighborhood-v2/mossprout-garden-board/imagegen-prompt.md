# Mossprout tall garden board - v2 art direction

Use `geometry-guide-v2-1024x1536.png` as the structural reference and the
runtime Home and Mossprout Stage 2 tiles only for camera, materials, lighting,
and toy proportions. Diagram colours and labels are not physical objects.

Create one continuous open garden island spanning two vertically stacked
flat-top hex cells. Preserve the tall-octagon footprint and all four diagonal
end-edge ports. The upper-right port joins Home, the lower-left joins
Mossprout, and the other two remain valid future joins. The long side edges are
smooth natural grass-over-cliff edges with no connection.

The green top is one broad, uninterrupted lawn extending to a soft beveled
outer lip. It has no wall, hedge perimeter, fence, rail, parapet, curb, raised
border, retaining ring, enclosure, middle seam, stairs, or bridge. Keep roughly
75 percent clear for a future 6x7 merge board.

Style: cozy premium Katchimeras 3D toy diorama; very low detail and very low
texture detail; chunky clay-like forms; soft broad bevels; matte materials;
large clean colour fields; simple rounded silhouettes; warm upper-left light.
Avoid realistic foliage, individual grass blades, leaf-by-leaf ferns, soil
noise, cracks, pebbles, tiny flowers, particles, and other micro-detail.

Use only a few large perimeter props: three rounded moss mounds, one small
smooth pond, one chunky stump seat, two simple lanterns, and at most four plant
clumps built from a few broad leaves. The shallow tan cliff uses about 8-12
large rounded clay masses. No characters, creatures, eggs, buildings, grid,
gameplay pieces, text, UI, watermark, or neighboring tiles.

Output a centered portrait 2:3 asset on a perfectly uniform solid `#ff00ff`
chroma background with generous padding and no external shadow or background
lighting variation.
