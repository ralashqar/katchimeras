# Mossprout selector emblem — Heartseed Sanctuary

The active top-level Haven tile now uses the selected Heartseed Sanctuary concept. It was generated with the built-in image-generation workflow using the existing Mossprout selector composition plus the Baristabbit, Encora, and toy-style Mossprout environments as authoritative material references.

## Final prompt

Redraw the Heartseed Sanctuary concept from scratch in the Katchimeras project visual language. Keep one large glowing heart-shaped seed gently cupped by two broad roots as the central world symbol. Use a compact floating island, two simple water pools, one short stepping path, three or four oversized leaf clumps, two cream flowers, and no more than two lanterns. Render it as a cozy 3D toy diorama with very low detail, very low texture detail, cushiony rounded construction, broad soft bevels, chunky proportions, smooth matte clay and soft vinyl materials. Match the project environments' front-isometric near-orthographic camera, warm upper-left studio lighting, broad gradients, and gentle contact shadows. No dense foliage, ferns, scattered pebbles, bark grooves, realistic leaf veins, photorealism, excessive gloss, perimeter walls, character, egg, UI, or text.

## Production extraction

The selected review render was passed through a dedicated background-extraction edit. Only the pale exterior background was removed; the complete Heartseed island was retained on genuine transparent alpha. The result is centered on the existing 1024×1024 selector canvas and packaged at 1024px, 512px, and 256px.
