# Codex direct Mossprout 2x2 concept v2

Mode: Codex built-in image generation, reference-guided generation.

Reference:

`design/floating-neighborhood-v2/floating-mossprout-source.png`

## Prompt

Create a square 2x2 sheet with four equal unlabelled panels showing the same
persistent Mossprout floating hex island progressing through Levels 1–4 in
reading order. Treat the reference as authoritative for the cozy stylized 3D
rendering, isometric camera, island proportions, rock underside, centred front
stairs, rounded matte materials, and nature-home language.

Keep the island shell, camera, crop, scale, stairs, perimeter wall, lighting,
and viewpoint cloned between panels. Only the interior develops.

- Level 1: mostly open moss, a tiny rear-centre sprout, two large rocks, one
  watering can, and three simple oversized flowers.
- Level 2: the sprout becomes a small plant, with one low curved bed containing
  three oversized flowers, one log bench at rear-left, the watering can, and
  substantial open moss.
- Level 3: the plant becomes a small rounded tree at rear-centre, with a simple
  pond at rear-right, the bench at rear-left, two broad fern clumps, and only a
  few flowers.
- Level 4: the tree becomes one distinctive large magical tree, the pond glows
  gently, the bench remains, exactly two lanterns appear, and three or four
  oversized luminous plants complete the grove without clutter.

Every panel must contain one small subtle circular standing patch in the lower
third, centred immediately above the stairs. Its diameter is roughly one-sixth
of the interior width and just large enough for one Katchimera. Its lower edge
is a short step from the stair opening, with at most one broad stepping stone.
The pad must have the same size and location in every panel and must not become
a central platform.

Use progression through a few large meaningful landmarks, not multiplied
props. Preserve broad calm floor space around the pad and through the middle.
Match the reference's chunky rounded forms, broad bevels, smooth clay-like
surfaces, low-frequency detail, warm diffuse light, and restrained earthy
palette.

Use a perfectly uniform `#FF00FF` chroma-key background and thin clean gutters.
No creature, egg, text, labels, numbers, watermark, central path, flower
carpet, pebble scatter, many stepping stones, micro-foliage, detailed bark,
realistic texture, extra structures, clutter, or geometry drift.
