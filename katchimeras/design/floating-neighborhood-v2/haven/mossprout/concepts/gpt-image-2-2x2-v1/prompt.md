# Mossprout 2x2 progression concept v1

Mode used for this draft: built-in reference-guided image generation. A direct
`gpt-image-2` CLI rerun was attempted, but `OPENAI_API_KEY` was not configured
in the environment.

Reference image:

`design/floating-neighborhood-v2/floating-mossprout-source.png`

## Prompt

Create one square 2x2 image containing four equal, clearly separated,
unlabelled panels. Each panel shows the same Mossprout nature Haven floating
hex tile at one successive environment-upgrade level. Reading order is
top-left Level 1, top-right Level 2, bottom-left Level 3, bottom-right Level 4.

- Level 1: mostly bare and newly inhabited. Calm moss floor, one tiny rear
  sprout, one watering can, two or three large rocks, and only a few simple
  flowers.
- Level 2: a modest first garden. The rear sprout is larger, with one small
  curved planting bed, a few oversized flowers, and one simple log seat. Keep
  it spacious and restrained.
- Level 3: an established garden nook. A small rounded tree at rear-centre, a
  simple pond at rear-right, a log bench at rear-left, and two or three large
  fern masses. Richer, but with large quiet floor areas remaining.
- Level 4: a resolved enchanted grove. One signature rounded magical tree at
  rear-centre, a luminous simple pond at rear-right, the bench nook at
  rear-left, two warm lanterns, and a few oversized magical plants. Developed
  and special, but not densely cluttered.

In every panel, include one small circular standing area horizontally centred
in the lower part of the tile, immediately above the centred stairs entrance.
The circle is just large enough for one Katchimera to stand. Connect it to the
stairs with at most one or two broad stepping stones. Do not place the circle
in the geometric centre. Fill the middle of the tile naturally; do not leave a
large empty central void.

Match the reference closely: cozy premium stylized 3D mobile-game toy diorama,
chunky rounded forms, broad soft bevels, smooth matte clay-like materials,
low-frequency surfaces, warm diffuse studio lighting, and clean readable
silhouettes. Use the same three-quarter isometric camera, flat-top hex
footprint, deep tapered floating-island underside, centred front stairs, scale,
and padding in all four panels.

Use a perfectly flat solid `#FF00FF` chroma-key background in every panel and
gutter. Do not use magenta in the tile art. No creature, egg, labels, numbers,
text, watermark, clutter, carpet of flowers, many pebbles, many stepping
stones, micro-foliage, leaf-by-leaf canopies, detailed bark, realistic texture,
extra buildings, changed island geometry, moved stairs, or large central
platform.
