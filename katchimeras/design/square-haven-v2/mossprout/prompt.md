# Mossprout square island v2

Generated with the built-in image-generation workflow. The user's approved
square Mossprout reference is the strict structural and camera reference; the
previous runtime Mossprout environment is a theme and material reference only.

## Locked camera

- Centered, front-facing, orthographic-like three-quarter isometric view.
- 58-degree nominal elevation, zero horizontal yaw, and zero roll.
- Very weak perspective convergence; rear width is at least 94% of front width.
- The top plane is dominant while a shallow 18–22% block-cliff underside remains visible.
- Square canvas, centered symmetry, horizontal front edge, and complete silhouette.
- Runtime bridge alignment points share the same height and the front stair is centered.

The machine-readable source of truth is
`design/square-haven-v2/island-camera-contract.json`.

## Content and style

- Compact near-square Mossprout platform with softly rounded corners.
- Leaf-roof burrow at rear center, open circular resident platform, symmetrical
  shallow front water channels, centered front steps, benches, planters, rails,
  and warm lanterns.
- Side bridge decks, planks, ropes, and outer posts are not baked into the
  environment. Connecting bridges are rendered separately at runtime.
- Environment only: no Mossprout creature, tray, items, grid, UI, text, or extra island.
- Cozy premium 3D toy diorama, matte molded clay/plastic, broad cushion bevels,
  chunky simplified geometry, large clean color masses, and very low texture detail.
- Flat `#ff00ff` chroma background, removed locally for the alpha master.
