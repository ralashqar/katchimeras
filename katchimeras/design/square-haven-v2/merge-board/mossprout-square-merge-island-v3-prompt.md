# Mossprout square merge-board island v3

Generated with the built-in image-generation workflow from the approved v1
merge island, the approved Square Haven Mossprout environment, and the true
orthographic junction-platform reference.

## Production contract

- Preserve the v1 island's side rails, side posts, layered foliage, flowers,
  lower/front lanterns, front rail, centered front stair, cliff modules, visual
  density, palette, lighting, and molded-toy material language.
- Remove only the rear horizontal rail, rear-center stair, and two rear-corner
  lanterns so the separately rendered order platforms have a shallow clear
  attachment area.
- Do not expand that clearance into a deep empty terrace. Continue green edge
  stones and restrained foliage around the rear corners and along both sides.
- Use true orthographic elevated projection: opposing construction edges stay
  parallel, rear and front widths match, and the board inset has equal top and
  bottom widths.
- Keep the inset gridless and empty; cells, grid lines, mist, items, shadows,
  and interaction art remain separately rendered.
- Match the project's cozy 3D toy-diorama style, broad soft bevels, chunky
  modular forms, matte materials, warm lighting, and low texture detail.
- Generate over flat `#ff00ff`, remove it locally, and preserve the complete
  generated square canvas while packaging so the art and grid remain aligned.

The calibrated orthographic playfield is `(190, 230)` to `(834, 665)` in the
1024 master.
