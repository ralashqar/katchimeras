# Relicoon museum island generation prompt

## Reference roles

- Image 1: `mossprout-main-environment-1024.png`, strict structural reference
  for the footprint, camera, scale, wall, front opening, steps, and cliff depth.
- Image 2: live runtime `floating_neighborhood_v2_relicoon_hex_tile.webp`,
  thematic reference for the museum identity, palette, architecture, and props.

## Prompt

Create one standalone Relicoon museum-and-archive floating island. Replace only
Mossprout-specific architecture, pond, and garden dressing from Image 1 with a
restrained Relicoon environment inspired by Image 2.

Preserve Image 1's rounded-square footprint, elevated front-isometric camera,
apparent island scale, padding, deep layered floating cliff, full walled
perimeter, centered opening in the front wall, and short chunky stairway
descending over the front cliff. Place a compact cream-and-terracotta
museum/archive cottage along the rear edge with a broad rounded roof and a warm
open doorway. Keep the central area spacious with one simple circular sandstone
compass mosaic for a separately rendered resident.

Along the outer edges only, use a small number of large simple props from the
live Relicoon tile: two display cases, one pottery urn, one fossil sculpture,
one closed chest, one rolled map, and two warm lanterns. Do not bake a character,
animal, egg, bridge, grid, or UI into the art.

Match the current production style: cozy 3D toy diorama, matte clay-like
materials, broad soft bevels, chunky simplified forms, very low detail, and
extremely low texture detail. Use warm sandstone and cream, muted teal, soft
terracotta, caramel brown, and amber lantern light. Avoid realism, painterly
grain, scratches, tiny ornament, excessive props, busy stone texture, or
detailed glass reflections.

Render on a perfectly uniform flat `#ff00ff` chroma-key background. No
background shadow, gradient, texture, floor, reflection, sky, cloud, glow, or
lighting variation. Never use magenta inside the island. Keep the complete
silhouette crisp, unclipped, and evenly padded. No text, labels, logos,
watermark, extra island, character, creature, or bridge.
