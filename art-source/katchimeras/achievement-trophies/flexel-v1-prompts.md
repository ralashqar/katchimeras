# Flexel trophy generation prompts

Both sheets were generated with the built-in image generator using these references:

- `assets/images/katchimeras/cutouts/flexel.png` — primary character materials and palette
- `assets/images/katchimeras/cutouts/hooplet.png` — sibling sport texture and energy
- `assets/images/katchimeras/manual-journal/movement.webp` — existing movement-prop scale and finish

## Shared direction

Create isolated Flexel keepsakes matching the supplied premium cozy miniature 3D Katchimeras art: rounded handcrafted clay, woven fabric, rubber and painted metal; soft diffuse light; teal, deep navy, warm orange and cream. They are treasured objects from Flexel's exercise-and-sport world, never generic cups or medals. Use a perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` chroma-key background. No grid lines, cast/contact shadows, floor plane, reflections, characters, text, numbers, generic awards, photorealism, glossy plastic, watermark, or magenta inside objects.

Every subject must occupy no more than 68% of its cell, remain centered, and keep every object and decoration wholly inside a completely empty uninterrupted chroma gutter.
