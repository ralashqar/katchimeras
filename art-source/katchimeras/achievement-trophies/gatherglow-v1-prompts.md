## Shared production prompt

Create isolated premium soft rounded 3D Katchimeras keepsakes. Warm honey amber, golden orange, cream, caramel wood and orange-cream checks; opaque ceramic, woven thread, carved wood and amber enamel. No transparent glass or glow haze. Use a perfectly flat uniform `#ff00ff` background. Exact grid; one compact connected object per cell; subject no more than 68% of its cell; at least 10% uninterrupted empty gutter on every side. Nothing touches a boundary. No cast/contact shadows, floor, reflections, text, numbers, characters, panels, scenery, generic medals or trophy cups.
