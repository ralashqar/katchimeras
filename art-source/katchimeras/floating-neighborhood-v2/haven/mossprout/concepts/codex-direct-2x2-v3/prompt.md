# Codex direct Mossprout 2x2 concept v3

Mode: Codex built-in image generation, using two visual references.

- Geometry reference: `design/floating-neighborhood-v2/floating-neutral-source.png`
- Mossprout style reference: `design/floating-neighborhood-v2/floating-mossprout-source.png`

## Prompt

Create a square 2x2 sheet of four equal unlabelled panels showing the same
floating hex island across four dramatically different restoration levels.
Use the neutral reference as authoritative for island geometry, camera, stairs,
shell and scale. Use the Mossprout reference for the cozy stylized 3D nature
language, but do not copy its mature leafy arch into early levels.

The contrast between Level 1 and Level 4 must be unmistakable at thumbnail
size. Clone the island shell, camera, crop, stairs, perimeter, underside,
lighting direction and scale between panels. Only the interior evolves.

- Level 1 — Neglected bare clearing: at least 55% broad exposed warm earth,
  sparse dull moss, one tiny two-leaf sprout at rear-centre, exactly two plain
  rocks, one old watering can and at most one tiny flower cluster. No tree,
  leafy arch, bench, pond, lantern, garden bed or mushrooms.
- Level 2 — First signs of growth: about 35% exposed earth, recovering moss,
  the sprout becoming a small plant, one curved bed with three oversized
  flowers, one log seat and the watering can. No tree canopy or pond.
- Level 3 — Established garden nook: rich moss with only two small earth
  patches, a medium young rounded tree at rear-centre, pond at rear-right, log
  bench at rear-left, two fern clumps, several large flowers and a mushroom
  pair. Keep calm floor around the character pad.
- Level 4 — Lush enchanted grove: fully restored deep green environment with a
  large ancient rounded signature tree and glowing hollow, larger luminous
  pond with a tiny waterfall, lush grouped fern masses, generous clusters of
  large flowers, mossy rocks, mushrooms, two lanterns and subtle glowing
  plants. Rich and celebratory without realistic micro-detail.

Every panel contains the same small circular standing patch in the lower third,
centred immediately above the front stairs. Its diameter is about one-sixth of
the interior width, its lower edge sits close to the stairs, and it has at most
one broad stepping stone. Never place it in the geometric centre.

Match the references' premium toy-diorama rendering: chunky rounded forms,
broad bevels, smooth matte clay-like surfaces, simplified low-frequency
textures, soft diffuse light and clean silhouettes.

Use a perfectly flat `#FF00FF` chroma-key background with thin white gutters.
No creature, egg, text, labels, numbers, watermark, freeform furniture,
buildings, central platform, long path, tiny pebble scatter, grass-blade
texture, realistic bark, leaf-by-leaf canopy or island geometry drift.
