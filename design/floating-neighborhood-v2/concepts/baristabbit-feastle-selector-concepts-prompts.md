## Production packaging

Each selected render was reprocessed with the built-in image-generation tool into a uniform magenta chroma intermediate. The connected chroma background was converted to genuine alpha, edge spill was removed, and each environment was packaged as 1024, 512, and 256 pixel WebP tiers.
